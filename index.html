Grammar Rules

• Subject Verb Agreement: Use base verb for "I" (e.g., I give, NOT I gives).
• Adverbs: BEFORE main verb.
(e.g., I never give flowers)
• Expressions: END of sentence.
(e.g., I give flowers once a week)
